About Me

I have been playing Dungeons and Dragons 5th edition for almost 4 years, and I have been DMing for 2.

My favorite 5e classes are:

  1. Warlock
    • They have the most unique class abilities
    • Can't be replicated through feats well
    • Great for multiclassing
  2. Sorcerer
    • Metamagic fuckin rocks
    • Excellent for multiclassing
    • Built-in adventure hook
  3. Rogue
    • Excellent utility skills
    • The 2nd most versatile of all the PHB classes (Bard is the most versatile)
    • Alright in combat, indispensible out of combat, especially if they have high CHA
  4. Wizard
    • Biggest spell list by a long shot
    • Ritual casting is such a lifesaver with resource management
    • THE utility caster
    • I wish they got more unique class abilities
    • Their subclasses are not that exciting

My favorite source book is Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, and my least favorite is the 5e Spelljammer material (It's genuinely terrible). My favorite D&D podcast is Legends of Avantris. My favorite free source website is rpgbot. My D&D hot takes are that every caster should take a couple levels in Fighter, for Action Surge, armor proficiencies, and better hit dice, and that pencil and paper is vastly superior to D&D Beyond.

As a DM, I generally go by Rule of Cool over what is strictly outlined in the rules. I also tend to end up focusing on "Is everybody having fun?" over "Are we actually progressing in the story at all?", so my campaigns usually end up very slow and often get sidetracked.

I do allow homebrew for your characters, and my only rules for it are 1. You can provide all of the rules for it, 2. It's not overpowered/only for the purpose of powerscaling, and therefore not fun, and 3. You are open to revising it if some part of it turns out to be overpowered and not fun.
